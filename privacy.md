
# Privacy Policy

**Team Organizer: Capacity & Sprint Planning**

**Last updated: November 2, 2026**

## 1. Who We Are

Team Organizer ("the App") is an Atlassian Forge application developed and maintained by its publisher.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes when installed on an Atlassian Cloud site, how that information is used, and how users can contact us regarding privacy matters.

Contact: emredelice100@gmail.com

## 2. Scope

This policy covers the Team Organizer application only.

It does not cover Atlassian products and services, which are governed by the [Atlassian Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 3. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the App may process the following information through Atlassian APIs:

- Jira issue keys, summaries, statuses, workflow states and estimation fields.
- Assignee display names and Atlassian account identifiers.
- Project, board, backlog and sprint identifiers and related planning information.
- JQL queries and issue information retrieved through supported import methods.
- Worklog and actual-hour information, only when the user explicitly selects the relevant worklog import feature.
- Team configuration, planning data, effort allocations and application preferences.

The App does not collect or store users' email addresses.

The App does not read Jira issue comments or attachments.

Since Atlassian account identifiers and display names may relate to identifiable individuals, some information processed by the App may constitute personal data.

The App does not intentionally request special-category personal data, payment information or government identification numbers.

## 4. How We Use Information

The information is processed exclusively to provide the application's capacity planning and sprint planning features.

This includes displaying Jira information, configuring teams, planning workloads, calculating effort allocations and retaining planning records.

When a user explicitly selects the Apply Plan to Jira action, the App may:

- Assign Jira issues to team members.
- Update original estimates.
- Create subtasks where supported by the planning workflow.
- Associate planned issues with the selected sprint.

These operations are initiated by the user and subject to the permissions of the requesting Jira user.

The App does not use customer data for advertising, profiling, selling information or training machine-learning models.

## 5. Hosting and Data Storage

The App runs on the Atlassian Forge platform.

Application configuration, planning data, effort allocations and related records are stored using Atlassian Forge storage associated with the relevant installation.

The developer does not operate a separate application server for storing customer planning data.

The application relies on Atlassian's platform infrastructure and security controls.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent customer data.

The App does not integrate with advertising networks or external analytics providers.

Atlassian provides the underlying cloud hosting and platform services. Atlassian's own privacy and data handling practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Application data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality.

When the App is uninstalled, associated Forge storage is handled according to Atlassian's Forge app data lifecycle and deletion procedures.

Customers may contact us regarding data access or deletion requests.

## 8. Security

The App operates within Atlassian Forge and relies on Atlassian's authentication, authorization and platform security mechanisms.

The App uses the permissions required for its documented functionality and operates subject to the requesting user's Jira permissions.
